Execution model

Five framework stages teams can keep or replace.

1Receive

Folder and webhook connectors normalize inbound work into RequestItem records.

2Understand

The AI layer classifies intent and extracts structured context.

3Propose

Workflow routing creates reviewable action payloads.

4Approve

Humans approve, edit, reject, or retry actions before execution.

5Execute

Executors dry-run locally or deliver normalized payloads to downstream tools.
